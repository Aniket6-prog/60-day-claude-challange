🚩 AI Red Flag Detector for Job Seekers

Analyze any Job Description and Company Information to identify hiring risks, toxic workplace signals, unrealistic expectations, and hidden red flags before you apply.

📌 Overview

Applying for jobs without thoroughly analyzing the Job Description (JD) can lead to burnout, poor work-life balance, misleading remote opportunities, or unstable companies.

AI Red Flag Detector helps job seekers evaluate opportunities objectively by identifying warning signs hidden inside job descriptions and company information.

Simply provide:

📄 Job Description
🏢 Company Information

The AI generates a comprehensive risk analysis with actionable insights and interview questions.

✨ Features
🔍 1. Unrealistic Requirements Detection

Identifies:

Excessive years of experience
Impossible skill combinations
Contradictory requirements
Multiple jobs disguised as one role
Inflated expectations

Examples:

"5+ years for Junior Developer"
"Full Stack + DevOps + UI/UX + Data Science"
"MBA Preferred for Entry-Level"
🚨 2. Toxic Workplace Signal Detection

Detects phrases commonly associated with burnout:

Fast-paced environment
Rockstar developer
Ninja programmer
Wear many hats
Self-starter
Hustle culture
Do whatever it takes
Work hard, play hard

Also checks for:

Poor work-life balance
Undefined responsibilities
Always available expectations
🌍 3. Remote Job Authenticity Check

Finds misleading remote claims such as:

Remote but relocation required
Remote with mandatory office attendance
Hidden travel expectations
Time-zone restrictions
Country-specific limitations
⚠️ 4. Hiring Risk Analysis

Evaluates:

Missing salary information
Vague responsibilities
Undefined success metrics
Excessive qualifications
Continuous hiring
Unrealistic interview process
🏢 5. Company Risk Assessment

Reviews available company information to identify:

Reputation concerns
Layoffs
High employee turnover
Funding concerns
Leadership instability
Glassdoor warning signs
Growth vs. instability indicators
📊 Output Includes
✅ Overall Risk Score (0–100)

Example:

72 / 100
High Risk

Generates personalized interview questions to validate identified risks.

Example:

Can you describe the team's work-life balance?
Why is this role currently open?
How is performance measured?
Are there any hidden travel or relocation expectations?
