# ⚽ Football Intelligence Hub

An AI-powered **Football Intelligence Experience** that combines **World Cup prediction analytics**, **adaptive football quizzes**, and **personality assessment** into one interactive workflow.

---

# Overview

Football Intelligence Hub is designed to provide users with a personalized football experience using AI-driven reasoning and structured sports analysis.

Instead of simply answering football questions, the application evaluates user knowledge, predicts tournament outcomes, measures football awareness, and identifies playing personality traits inspired by football legends.

---

# Features

## Stage 0 – Knowledge Level Assessment

The experience begins by determining how familiar the user is with football.

The selected knowledge level is used to:

* Adjust explanation depth
* Simplify or increase terminology
* Adapt quiz difficulty
* Personalize recommendations

---

## Stage 1 – FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Report

Generates a comprehensive prediction report using workbook data.

Includes:

* Most Likely Winner
* Runner-Up
* Dark Horse Team
* Players to Watch
* Confidence Scores (0–100%)
* Supporting Evidence
* Risk Analysis
* Factors Against Each Prediction

---

## Stage 2 – Football IQ Quiz

Interactive multiple-choice assessment that measures football knowledge.

Output includes:

* Football Awareness Score (0–100)
* Fan Classification
* Strongest Knowledge Areas
* Weakest Areas
* Learning Recommendations

Fan classifications include:

* Beginner Fan
* Casual Viewer
* Football Follower
* Football Enthusiast
* Football Expert

---

## Stage 3 – Messi vs Ronaldo Personality Match

A personality assessment that avoids directly asking users which player they prefer.

The quiz evaluates:

* Ambition
* Discipline
* Leadership
* Teamwork
* Creativity
* Competitiveness
* Confidence
* Work Ethic
* Learning Style
* Decision-Making Style
* Big-Match Mentality
* Emotional Control
* Adaptability

Results include:

* Messi Compatibility %
* Ronaldo Compatibility %
* Personality Explanation
* Shared Traits
* Decision-Making Analysis
