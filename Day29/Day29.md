🚀 Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

An interactive, story-driven Supply Chain Crisis Management Simulator built with React, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Players take on the role of a supply chain executive, navigate unexpected disruptions, negotiate with suppliers, answer leadership challenges, and invest in AI solutions to improve business resilience.

✨ Features
🎲 Random company generation
🌍 Dynamic crisis scenarios
⚔️ Interactive War Room decision-making
🤝 Four-round supplier negotiation
👨‍💼 CEO Boardroom leadership assessment
🤖 AI strategy investment simulator
📈 Animated business KPIs
📊 Executive performance dashboard
🔄 Replay with randomized outcomes
📱 Fully responsive premium dark UI
