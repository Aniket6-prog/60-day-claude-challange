# 🎓 Study Planner AI

An AI-powered study planning assistant that helps students organize their learning, prioritize subjects, and generate structured study schedules using intelligent planning.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📋 Smart Study Planning

* Personalized AI-generated study plans
* Goal-based planning
* Weekly and daily schedules
* Subject prioritization
* Adaptive workload balancing

### 📂 Input Support

* Free text
* Form-based inputs
* Uploaded syllabus or notes
* Multi-turn AI conversations

### 📊 Dashboard

* Study progress overview
* Daily task tracking
* Weekly planner
* Subject distribution
* Learning insights

### 🤖 AI Features

* Intelligent study recommendations
* Time management suggestions
* Revision planning
* Focus area identification
* Personalized learning strategies

## 📚 How It Works

1. Enter your study goals.
2. Add exam dates and available study hours.
3. Upload syllabus or study notes.
4. Configure priorities and learning preferences.
5. Generate an AI-powered personalized study plan.
