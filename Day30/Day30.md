# 🚚 Supply Chain Builder

An interactive, beginner-friendly **Supply Chain Management Simulator** built as a **single HTML file** using **React (CDN), HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

The application teaches supply chain concepts by allowing users to make real business decisions and instantly see how those choices impact company performance.

## ✨ Features

* Random company generation
* Beginner-friendly learning experience
* Step-by-step supply chain building
* Supplier selection
* Factory location planning
* Warehouse strategy selection
* Transportation mode selection
* Inventory strategy management
* Live business metrics dashboard
* Overall Supply Chain Score (0–100)
* Strengths and weaknesses analysis
* Biggest business risk identification
* Practical improvement recommendations
* Premium dark UI
* Responsive design
* Smooth animations
* Replay functionality

## 📊 Business Metrics

* Cost Efficiency
* Delivery Speed
* Supply Chain Risk
* Customer Satisfaction
* Sustainability

## 🧠 Learning Outcomes

This simulator helps users understand:

* How suppliers impact business resilience
* Factory location trade-offs
* Warehouse planning strategies
* Transportation mode comparisons
* Inventory management decisions
* Cost vs Speed trade-offs
* Risk management
* Customer experience optimization
* Sustainable supply chain practices

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* React (CDN)
* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript (ES6)
* Babel

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Download the project.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3. No installation, backend, or internet connection is required after the libraries are available.
4. Start building your supply chain.

## 🎯 Project Goal

To make supply chain management easy to understand through interactive decision-making, live business metrics, and practical feedback instead of traditional lectures.
