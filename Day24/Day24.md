🚀 AI Startup Business Strategy & Sustainability Analyzer

An AI-powered business consulting framework that evaluates whether a startup idea can become a sustainable business.

Instead of focusing only on product features, the system analyzes business fundamentals including revenue, distribution, customer acquisition, pricing strategy, and investment readiness.
