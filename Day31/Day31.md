# 🚨 AI Supply Chain Control Tower

An interactive **Operations Management Simulator** that places you in the role of **Head of Operations** for a global supply chain company.

Your objective is to manage disruptions, protect revenue, maintain customer satisfaction, and maximize operational performance before time runs out.

## 🌟 Features

* Real-time Operations Control Dashboard
* Live KPI Monitoring
* Dynamic Supply Chain Alerts
* Decision-Based Gameplay
* Multiple Operational Scenarios
* Priority-Based Alert Management
* Countdown Timer
* Increasing Difficulty
* Live Event Log
* Performance Scoring System
* Final Performance Grade (A+ to D)
* Responsive Dark UI
* Pause Game
* Help & Instructions
* Visual Sound Toggle
* Replay Functionality

## 📈 KPIs

* Service Level
* Customer Satisfaction
* Inventory Health
* Transportation Efficiency
* Operating Cost
* Revenue Protected
* Overall Score
* Remaining Time

## 🚨 Operational Alerts

The simulator randomly generates real-world supply chain disruptions such as:

* Port Congestion
* Supplier Delay
* Truck Breakdown
* Warehouse Stockout
* Customs Inspection
* Demand Spike
* Factory Machine Failure
* Weather Disruption
* Wrong Inventory Count
* Damaged Shipment

Each alert includes:

* Priority Level
* Time Remaining
* Business Impact
* Recommended Actions

## 🎮 Gameplay

As the **Head of Operations**, you must:

* Prioritize critical issues
* Select the best response
* Balance cost, speed, and customer satisfaction
* Protect revenue
* Reduce operational risk
* Adapt to increasing alert frequency

Every decision has immediate and, in some cases, delayed consequences that affect your overall performance.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Vanilla JavaScript

**No external libraries, frameworks, APIs, or backend services are used.** Everything runs in a single offline HTML file.

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

This simulator helps users understand:

* Supply Chain Management
* Operations Strategy
* Logistics Planning
* Inventory Management
* Transportation Decisions
* Risk Mitigation
* KPI Monitoring
* Business Trade-offs
* Real-Time Decision Making

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3. Start managing your supply chain operations.

No installation or setup required.

## 💡 Project Vision

The **AI Supply Chain Control Tower** demonstrates how interactive simulations can make complex operations and supply chain concepts easier to understand through hands-on decision-making, real-time analytics, and engaging gameplay.
