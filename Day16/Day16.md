# 📈 Stock Fundamental Research Analyzer

An AI-powered **Stock Fundamental Research Analyzer** that generates structured, evidence-based research reports for Indian and global listed companies using publicly available financial information.

The project focuses on helping users understand a company's **business quality, financial performance, valuation, competitive advantages, growth prospects, and risks**—without providing investment recommendations.

> **Disclaimer:** This project is built for educational and research purposes. It does **not** provide buy, sell, or hold recommendations, target prices, or personalized investment advice.

---

# 🚀 Features

* Company Overview
* Current Market Price (CMP)
* Market Capitalization

* 52-Week High & Low
