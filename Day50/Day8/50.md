# 🛡️ Defend Your Experience

> **Don't just claim your experience. Be ready to defend it.**

**Defend Your Experience** is an AI-powered adaptive interview preparation application designed to challenge the claims users make about their own experience.

Unlike traditional resume tools, this project does **not** focus on rewriting or optimizing a resume.

Instead, it acts as an intelligent skeptic.

It asks increasingly specific questions about projects, technical decisions, responsibilities, challenges, evidence, and measurable outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Candidates often prepare answers for common interview questions but struggle when an interviewer asks:

* Why did you choose this approach?
* What exactly did you contribute?
* How do you know it worked?
* What evidence supports that claim?
* What was the hardest part?
* What would you do differently?
* What happened when your approach failed?

**Defend Your Experience** is designed to simulate exactly this pressure.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📄 Experience Upload

Upload documents describing your experience, including:

* Resume / CV
* Portfolio
* Project descriptions
* LinkedIn exports
* Research work
* Performance reviews
* Startup stories
* Freelance experience

### 🔍 Claim Identification

Meaningful claims are extracted and converted into potential interview challenges.

### 🤖 Adaptive AI Interviewer

The interviewer doesn't follow a fixed questionnaire.

Every answer can influence the next question.

The conversation can move from:

**General claim → Evidence → Personal contribution → Technical depth → Challenge → Impact**

### 🎯 Weak Spot Detection

The application identifies areas such as:

* Vague explanations
* Missing evidence
* Weak technical depth
* Lack of measurable impact
* Unclear personal contribution

### 📊 Defense Analytics

Track:

* Confidence score
* Claims identified
* Questions answered
* Weak spots
* Evidence quality
* Specificity
* Overall defense progress

### 📑 Defense Report

Generate a final assessment showing:

* Strong claims
* Weak claims
* Confidence level
* Areas requiring preparation
* Recommended improvements

### 💾 Local Session Storage

Interview progress can be preserved using browser `localStorage`.

### 📤 Export

Export session information as a JSON Defense Report.

### 📱 Responsive Design

Designed for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

---

## 🧠 Core Philosophy

Traditional interview preparation asks:

> "What questions might they ask?"

This project asks:

> **"Can you defend every claim you've already made?"**

That difference changes the entire preparation process.

```text
User Experience Document
```

## 💡 Example Use Case

A candidate writes:

> "Built a scalable web application using Java and Spring Boot."

The AI should not simply accept the claim.

It can challenge:

**"What made the application scalable?"**

Then:

**"Which architectural decision had the biggest impact?"**

Then:

**"How did you measure scalability?"**

Then:

**"What bottleneck did you encounter?"**

The goal is to move from a **statement** to **defensible evidence**.
