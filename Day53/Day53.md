# 💰 FinBuddy

### Smart Expense Tracker for Students

FinBuddy is a full-stack web application designed to help students understand and manage their personal finances.

The project is being developed as part of the **AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge** through a focused 10-day capstone journey from product discovery to deployment.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Students often struggle to understand where their money goes.

Expenses are frequently scattered across:

- Notes
- Banking applications
- Spreadsheets
- Memory
- Messaging apps

This makes it difficult to understand spending patterns, maintain budgets, and work toward financial goals.

---

## 💡 Solution

FinBuddy provides a simple student-focused platform for:

- Tracking income and expenses
- Organizing transactions
- Managing monthly budgets
- Setting financial goals
- Viewing financial summaries
- Receiving AI-powered financial insights
