Concept 01

The Headline Gap

Why do headlines rarely match the story?

In the attention economy, a headline's primary job is no longer to inform you—it is to capture your click.

This leads to the "Headline Gap": the distance between the exaggerated promise of a title and the mundane reality of the article. Over time, reading only sensational headlines distorts our view of reality, making the world seem more dangerous, polarized, and extreme than it actually is.

Concept 02

Emotional Hijacking

How algorithms and creators exploit your nervous system.

Content that triggers high-arousal emotions—specifically outrage, fear, and awe—is shared exponentially faster than neutral content.

Bad actors and engagement-hungry creators weaponize language to bypass your critical thinking and directly stimulate your fight-or-flight response. When you feel a sudden spike of anger or anxiety while scrolling, you are being manipulated.
