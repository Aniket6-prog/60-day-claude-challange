# 📈 Think Like a Marketing Strategist: Grow This Brand

An interactive React-based learning simulator that teaches beginners how marketing strategists think.

Unlike traditional AI marketing tools that simply generate content, this application focuses on **decision-making**, **critical thinking**, and **marketing fundamentals** through an engaging, scenario-based experience.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🏢 Business Mode

Create a marketing strategy for your own business.

### 🙋 Personal Brand Mode

Build a personal brand using your expertise, story, and niche.

### 🎲 Random Client Generator

Practice with randomly generated businesses, audiences, budgets, competitors, and marketing challenges.

### 🎯 Audience Understanding

Learn why identifying the right audience is the foundation of every successful marketing strategy.

### 🌐 Platform Selection

Choose the best social media platforms and understand why each platform fits (or doesn't fit) your target audience.

### 🧱 Content Pillars

Select three strategic content pillars that align with your marketing goals.

### 📅 30-Day Growth Roadmap

Receive a weekly strategic marketing plan instead of individual content ideas.

### ⚡ Unexpected Marketing Events

Respond to realistic marketing scenarios such as viral content, competitor campaigns, podcast invitations, or public criticism.

### 📊 Growth Report

Get a complete analysis of your marketing decisions, including:

* Audience Understanding
* Platform Strategy
* Content Strategy
* Growth Potential
* Best Decision
* Biggest Mistake
* Three Key Marketing Lessons

### 🤖 Prompt Engineering

Every major section includes reusable **"How to Ask Claude"** prompts, helping users learn effective AI prompting alongside marketing strategy.
